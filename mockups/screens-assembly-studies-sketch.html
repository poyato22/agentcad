Revision E · roomier top bar

Same greyscale + blue system. Screen 1 is the main workspace now living inside an assembly: you focus one part while its neighbours stay visible as ghosts and its mating faces are locked. Screen 2 is the Studies tab for heavy analysis: topology optimization, generative design, CFD wind tunnel, with a choice of solver (open-source local, Ansys, or cloud). Screen 3 is Sketch: draw roughly, Claude turns it into a constrained part.

1 · Design — focus a part inside the assembly

The left panel is now the assembly tree with the focused part's feature tree nested inside. "In context" mode shows the handlebar, clamp and phone cradle as grey ghosts. Faces that mate with other parts are pinned with a lock, and agents can't change them without asking. Changes are checked against the whole build for collisions.

2 · Studies — topology optimization, generative design, CFD

Each study shows which solver runs it. Light ones run locally on open-source solvers. Heavy ones can go to Ansys (through its Python API) or a cloud solver. An agent sets up each study, runs it, and turns the result back into editable Onshape features.

Draw with mouse, trackpad or Apple Pencil, or drop in a photo of a paper napkin sketch. Scribbled numbers and notes count. Claude shows how it read your sketch side by side and asks only about what it couldn't tell, before it builds anything in Onshape.
